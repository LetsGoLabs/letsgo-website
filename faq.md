## AI Guidelines 
### What AI models does LetsGo Labs use? 
LetsGo Labs uses its own AI models, and may use production or preview models from Anthropic, Google, OpenAI, Perplexity. Sometimes these models produce unexpected results or errors. LetsGo Slides is designed to detect and recover gracefully from AI generation errors, without affecting your presentation. If an operation produces an unexpected result or an error, then retry the operation and it will often work as expected the second time.  

### Can I trust the results?
AI excels at automating tasks, analyzing datasets, and identifying patterns, but lacks the nuanced judgement, creativity, and ethical reasoning of humans. AI also makes mistakes, so we recommend you double check everything it creates. Critical thinking helps bridge this gap, ensuring that AI outputs are contextualized, questioned, and applied effectively. 

LetsGo Slides improves the accuracy of the content AI produces by using internet search to research your content, then using AI to format the research as slides. Any facts presented are provided with citations (in the speaker notes), that links to the fact source. LetsGo Slides also includes a Fact Check feature that analyzes the facts and assertions presented on a slide, and checks the accuracy against credible sources.

### Why do I get error messages that say the content can not be displayed?
AI models have rules limiting them from creating certain types of content or responding to certain prompts. When you get an error message like this, it means the AI interprets your request as a request for it to do something it is not allowed to do, and blocks you from seeing the results (this is known as “Responsible AI”). Sometimes you can try again, sometimes you need to make changes to your content before creating text or images. 

Here are some of the rules that our current models must follow:
* No pornographic or offensive images
* No images that involve copyrighted content
* No hate-speech, dangerous or abusive content

If you try to create content that is prohibited under the Responsible AI guidelines, you will be presented with an error. The error message is preventative, and does not affect you from trying again. You can attempt the same operation again, or adjust your content before trying again. You will be charged the credits it took to generate the image or content, even when the action is blocked.

## Privacy
### Is my data safe?
LetsGo Slides works entirely within the trusted Google and Microsoft platforms, without any external dependencies or sharing of information. We respect your existing security permissions and do not train any AI models with your data. This is a key advantage of using LetsGo Slides rather than going out to another general purpose AI service and uploading your data/content. With LetsGo Slides, you stay within your proven and trusted ecosystem. 

Lets Go Slides does everything in our power to respect your privacy. For example: your metadata is cached for up to six hours then destroyed. Images are removed from storage after 30 days of inactivity.

# LetsGo Slides for Microsoft PowerPoint™

## Do more with Microsoft PowerPoint™ 
LetsGo Slides brings the power of artificial intelligence into Microsoft PowerPoint™. With LetsGo Slides, you can create slides, add AI-generated images, or reword and translate text with a single click, all without leaving your slide presentation.

LetsGo Slides is available in the Microsoft Marketplace. Every new account comes with free credits to get you started.

## Presentations
LetsGo Slides can create an entire presentation based on the text from your presentation title. Each presentation includes original text, images, speakers notes and citations, and professional-quality formatting. 

Once your presentation is created, you can continue to use LetsGo Slides to modify the layout, rewrite or translate text, and generate alternative images on any slide. 

With LetsGo Slides, you never have to start with a blank title slide again. 

## Fact Check
Working on a presentation, compiling research or proving a point to your annoying brother. LetsGo Slides can help with that.

Using the Fact Checker is simple. Navigate to the slide with the information you want to check, open the Fact Check from the LetsGo Slides sidebar and click start. 

Each assertion on the slide is researched, and rated 0-100% for factual accuracy. LetsGo Slides then displays it all in one sidebar view. 

Click on the assertion in the sidebar and it opens an expander that shows the reasoning and citations behind the fact check. 

## Image Generation 
LetsGo Slides has the most intuitive AI image generation experience out there. Instead of  writing prompts, you simply click a button and LetsGo Slides generates an image based on the content of your slide.

LetsGo Slides automatically detects the dimensions of the slide and the optimal aspect ratio for the image and handles it all for you automatically. You also get to choose from normal, fast, and high quality AI models to create your images. 

All the images you create with LetsGo Slides are yours to keep. We make it easy for you to share them with a link, post on LinkedIn, or download to your computer.
